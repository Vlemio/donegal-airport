The approach

One of the most scenic approaches in the world.

Errigal to the east, the open Atlantic to the west. Pilots and passengers descend over the Rosses with the Tory Island ferry visible below. There is no approach like it on the island of Ireland.

The story of CFN

A grass strip. A Christmas Eve flight. Forty-seven years.

From a 610-metre grass runway on a sandbar to Ireland's first ACA Level 4 carbon-accredited airport. Malinair, Ryanair, offshore helicopters for Corrib, and one runway extended three times.
