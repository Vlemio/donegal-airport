The approach

Ranked among the most scenic approaches in the world.

Errigal to the east, the Atlantic to the west. Pilots descend over the Rosses with the Tory Island ferry below and the Donegal coast stretching north to Malin Head. There is no approach like it.

The story

A grass strip. A lawyer. An £85 ticket on Christmas Eve.

From a sandbar between the Atlantic and Mount Errigal to Ireland's first carbon-neutral airport. Forty-seven years of routes opened and abandoned, helicopters for oil rigs, and one runway extended three times.
