Sustainability  ·  21 April 2026

ACA Level 4 Transformation confirmed — a first for Ireland.

Level 4 is the highest tier in airport carbon accreditation. No airport on this island had reached it before.

Donegal Airport became the first airport in Ireland to achieve Level 4 Transformation certification in the Airport Carbon Accreditation programme. The programme runs from Level 1 to Level 4 — most airports stop at Level 2 or 3.

Level 4 requires more than internal improvements. It asks airports to extend accountability across the full supply chain — airlines, ground handlers, concessions, contractors. Achieving it requires independent audit, not self-declaration.

The certification is backed by concrete targets: a 70% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 2030, and net-zero across all emission scopes by 2050. Ongoing initiatives include LED lighting upgrades across the terminal, solar PV installation, and electric vehicle deployment on the apron.

The accreditation is issued by Airports Council International. It reflects years of staff and stakeholder commitment — and will require the same to maintain.
